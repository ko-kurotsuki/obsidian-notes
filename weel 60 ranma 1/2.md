Very Tragic Story: With a bit of time and effort you are able to enchant
up to a small pool's worth of water to remember the first living thing to be submerged in it. With no drownings necessary! After that, anyone soaked by its water will be cursed to change into whatever was imprinted onto the "artificial Jusenkyo" (whether that be a human girl, a panda, or something much weirder) whenever they're splashed with cold water, only able to change back by using hot water, instead. You can always fix this by just making a pool of whatever they were origi-nally and having them jump in.
Omae Wa Mou: Pressure points, moxibustion, acupuncture. You have
gained a mastery of influencing the body and mind of others through these kind of precision techniques that would put any doctor to shame. Whether it's weakening a foe to the point were a child would be stronger, wiping away all memory of one specific person from someone's mind, or massively increasing a body's sensitivity, you have the knowledge to either cause or heal all of the above and oh so much more.
Hair Care Warfare: Your luck becomes truly impressive; whether that is
good or bad is debatable. When around those you find attractive lewd accidents and events become incredibly common for them, stuff like somehow getting into the bath at the same time, or their clothes being destroyed and/or lost would be everyday occurrences. Luckily, nobody notices this only happens around you. You are also amazing at attack-ing clothing without ever hitting the one wearing them.
