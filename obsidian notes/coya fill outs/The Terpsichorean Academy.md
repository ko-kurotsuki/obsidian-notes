RAKSHASI
Offspring of the Divine Beasts of the Near East, the rakshasi come in many flavours - and rule over their homeland with iron claws. Those who seek to become dancers are often more interested in the martial classes than dance itself, but their cute looks have made them popular all over Mundus.
Bonus:
Gain one Aspect Infusion for free

CURVACEOUS
Now this is a body built for the stage! It might not be as fast and agile as the rest, but it will be hard indeed to find a settlement without a crowd willing to pay good money to see your clothes fall... among other things.
Those things being sex.

FAT-CAT BRAT
Though your family wasn't noble, it was the next best thing: filthy rich. Whatever you wanted, you got, from the best tutors to the finest dresses. Should your dancer ambitions fall through, you have a comfy future to fall back on.
Bonus:
Gain the Extravagant Outfit for free

UNIFORM
The Academy uniform is a comfortable set woven from tafetta silk. A lightweight black top is worn alongside a loose skirt featuring decorative cloth-of-gold vines. Apprentices are encouraged to accessorise, and are free to choose their own footwear.
The uniform is to be worn at all times when inside of the Academy.

SKIMPY
Dancers are ladies' entertainers first and foremost, and with a skimpy outfit few will forget that fact while the dancer is around. Many such sets feature cleverly designed clasps to quickly remove pieces of fabric, and they usually come with veils, shawls, and similar items to cover up with - be it for a cold evening or a striptease.
EXTRAVAGANT
Ostentatious, screamy, and often quite impractical, extravagant outfits are for one thing only; drawing attention. Sewn from colourful fabrics and covered in feathers, gold, and gems, they are also quite popular among wealthier dancers. Many Dune Kingdom festivals feature dancing girls in similar getups, but even they pale to these outfits.
