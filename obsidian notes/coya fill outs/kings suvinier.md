
Churel
When you first found her, she had run out of the woods, begging for help and that bandits had tried to take her. You came to the burning cottage and found, of all things, your own soldiers. A band led by a depraved young nobleman, seeking to prove himself. You had them all executed, as an example to the rest of your men.
For whatever reason, the Etsaians believe that green hair is a bad omen. As such, most dubbed Churel a witch from birth. Witch being a term for evil, supposedly hellspawned magic users. Due to this, she lived in the woods alone after her parents died, until the war happened. Out of gratitude and having nowhere to go, Churel swore herself to you.
Churel adores you in ways few can match and loves that she is seen as beautiful in your realms. She readily displaying herself to taunt others that look on her lustfully, knowing that she belongs to you alone. She'd gladly serve you in public, even relishes it. She never had many gifts before,
so any you give her, she cherishes. Even the wholesome ones. Especially the wholesome ones.
