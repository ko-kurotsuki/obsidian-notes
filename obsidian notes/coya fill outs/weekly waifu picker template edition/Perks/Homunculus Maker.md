The Yggdmillenia clan's army is for the most part staffed by homunculi, artificial humans of their creation. With this perk, you gain the ability to make homunculus servants of your own, which also allows you to form bodies for those who haven't got one of their own. Do be aware though, the homunculi are NOT lifeless dolls, they can and WILL develop personality of their own.
