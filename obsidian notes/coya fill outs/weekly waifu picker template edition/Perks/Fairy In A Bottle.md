Should worst come to worst and you are dealt a mortal blow, this will allow you to be pulled back from the jaws of death with a bit of healing. You won't be back to 100%, you're still going to have to recover, but you won't be dead. There is a time limit on uses of this, but you will know when it's charged, when it's not and how much longer it'll take to charge.
