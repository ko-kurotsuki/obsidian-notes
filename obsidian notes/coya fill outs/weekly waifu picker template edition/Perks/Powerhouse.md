Chi, chakra, mana, whatever you want to call it, whatever the power system of the world you visit runs on, if it is reliant upon internally contained personal reservoirs of that power, your reservoir is now in no uncertain terms that of a TITAN. Your reserves will be equal to that of every other being capable of calling on this power system within a planet width radius of you, thus only absurd waste will even begin to strain your reserves, and feats requiring otherwise prohibitive amounts of power will be child's play for you.
