Your bodily fluids (saliva, blood, semen, etc) now taste delicious to any and everybody. They are also highly nutritious, such that so long as their supply is not cut off, someone could actually live off a diet of nothing but your fluids. They will also slow aging such that the consumer's aging will match yours should yours be at a slower rate.
