You can transform yourself into living electricity, zipping around through power lines and even into machines you can then control with some practice. Just be careful, you're not totally immune to other kinds of electricity and you can overload yourself or short out if you end up in water, returning you to your non-electricity form in the nearest available space. And as a side benefit, if you want you can style your hair up with static charge however you like, no gel required!
