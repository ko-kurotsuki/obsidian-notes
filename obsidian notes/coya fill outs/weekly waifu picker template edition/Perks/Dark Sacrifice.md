You'll never hit your ceiling, and you'll always be able to grow stronger and more potent no matter how long you live. Unfortunately, whether immense deeds of bravery and heroism or dark sacrifices of things that once held sentimental value for you, there'll always be a price to be paid to break through those last barriers. Can you hold on to your life and your soul?
