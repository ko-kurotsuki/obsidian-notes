You are now able to mentally interface with and project your consciousness into digital mainframes at will. You can't immediately control them, but you can gain an unparalleled understanding of them and if you project your consciousness into them, you can mentally interact with and manipulate their programming although this does take a bit of time and effort. This applies to any and all computers no matter how alien or advanced they may be.
