No matter how selfish or absurd your actual motivations may be, any action you take will be interpreted in the best way possible for you by any bystanders who don't know you or your motivations well enough to guess what's going on in your head at the time. Your friends won't be fooled, but anyone else will see whatever you're doing in the best light possible. Won't let you get away with crimes, but any sort of wacky shenanigans that could be logically seen in a good light will be, even if that logic is completely wrong...
