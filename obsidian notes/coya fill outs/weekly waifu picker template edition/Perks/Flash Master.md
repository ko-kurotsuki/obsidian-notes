If you've watched anime for any length of time, you've probably seen the flash step in action. You put a bit of power (magic, spirit energy, good ol' muscle) into your feet, push off, and move to a point you can see so fast you seem to be teleporting. With this perk you can not only do it but master it, out-speeding even other speed demons and reacting faster than the eye can follow, and the distance you can cover with each flash step is truly immense.
