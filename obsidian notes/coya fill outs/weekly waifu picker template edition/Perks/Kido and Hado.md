You've mastered ninety-nine spells of binding and destruction, respectively, with this perk. These can achieve a wide variety of potent and useful effects, from instantly putting an opponent in a supernatural armlock at the low end to summoning a bunch of giant energy dragons on the other. The spells can only be resisted or broken by sufficient spiritual power and can be made stronger with proper incantations, though using them without them can still be plenty effective. Normally each one needs practice; congrats on getting them all for free!
