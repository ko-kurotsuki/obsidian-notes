Your body can now create and store massive amounts of energy in response to your emotions. This energy will reinforce your body, allow you faster than light flight and you can launch the energy in varying degrees as a form of attack which will be devastating. Your body also now possesses the same properties as a small sun, so anything which can benefit or suffer from sunlight will experience those effects in your presence, while you will become immune to such things and all heat/radiation up to bathing in a star with impunity.
