When you get this perk, pick a reasonably-sized animal. You can turn into that animal at will. You can remain transformed indefinitely and still talk in a completely different voice, so it's actually potentially pretty good for disguising yourself, lying low, and sneaking around; nothing can reveal you're anything but that animal. You can also instead use the perk to transform your mind, becoming feral and bestial in thought but gaining great powers related to the animal you picked. Free advice, maybe don't stay this way too long...
