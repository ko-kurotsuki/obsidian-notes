An entire TEAM of lawyers could not prevent you from finding some absurd loophole in any agreement or the rules of any competition you may find yourself entering into. These loopholes will always turn whatever your situation is to your distinct and often flat out overwhelming advantage. Do be wary that this only really helps when the other person is willing to play by the rules.
