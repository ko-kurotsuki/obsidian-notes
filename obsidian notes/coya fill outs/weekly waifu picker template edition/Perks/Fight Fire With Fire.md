If you can obtain a sample of a certain type of power, you can grant people the ability to replicate it as naturally as if it were their own. Things like using a bit of a dragon's magic to grant a human power identical to that dragon, although this is not limited to dragons. Gods, demons, unicorns, whatever. You sample it, you can imbue it and fling it right back in their face.
