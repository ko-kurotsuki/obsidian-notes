Summon up to a hundred clones of yourself, which while capable of physically interacting with the world will become more fragile with every clone you make to the point where at a hundred clones a good shove is enough to disperse one. You receive the memories of the clones when they're dispersed, but the clones can do anything you can as long as they're around.
