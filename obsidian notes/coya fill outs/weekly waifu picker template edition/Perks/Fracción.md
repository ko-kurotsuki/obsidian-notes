A nice perk for when you don't wanna break up a friend group. If a close friend or lover has got a network of loyal, devoted followers, then they'll happily transfer some of that same loyalty and devotion to you, and follow you in the same way, so long as that loyalty and devotion is returned in kind. Yes, this means they'll come along if you spend a voucher on someone, and yes, it can potentially turn romantic if you like.
