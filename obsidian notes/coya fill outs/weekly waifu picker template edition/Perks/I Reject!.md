You gain the power to affect the physical world through rejection, preventing attacks, reversing injury, or even tearing apart enemies in direct proportion to how deeply you refuse to accept the outcome you're preventing. However, it is deeply tied to your personality; a nurturing type might be able to reverse even death but struggle to work up the outrage to really hurt someone, while an angrier person might find it easy to lash out but lack the fear to block.
