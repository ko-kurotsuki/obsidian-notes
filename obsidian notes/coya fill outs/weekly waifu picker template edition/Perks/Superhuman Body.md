Strength, Speed, Senses, and your general durability will be boosted through the roof. You'll be able to shrug off cannon fire, blades and most everything else. You're not completely indestructible, enough punishment will eventually wear you down, but unless you're standing there just taking it or up against something even stronger than you, very few people will ever get to see you reach your limit.
