You and anyone you care about will remain yourselves no matter what form you take whether that form was taken voluntarily or not. Also, you will always be recognized by those who love you if you are in a form not your own and be able to communicate with your loved ones even if you lack the physiology to do so. More importantly, any such malicious transformations can be dispelled by a kiss from someone who loves you. (sex works too but a kiss is more romantic)
