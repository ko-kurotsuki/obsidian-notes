You can now spawn various creatures from your nerve endings. You will have complete control of the creatures, their shape, actions, everything, and you will be able to feel whatever they do in the place where the nerve they were grown from was. Should a spawn be killed, the nerve it was connected to will simply regrow. Should YOU be killed, you will be able to respawn from one of your creatures should one still exist.
