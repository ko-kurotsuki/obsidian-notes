Lost in the Nature
Deep and unexplored jungles, freezing and deadly mountain peak or arid and vicious desert, your headquarters are sited in the middle of one of the most hostile natural places in the world.
Only the most courageous (or the craziest) adventurers are able to reach it. The infernal climate, the dangerous flora and the fierce fauna that permanently harass your assassins have made them the toughest survivors in the world, able to undertake a mission in the deadliest places.

Educated as a Killer
Members are recruited from childhood, whether orphans enrolled in the street or children abducted from their parents. Then, the guild provides them a proper education in their hideout, teaching them everything they have to know as future assassins. It takes tens of years to train an efficient hitman. In fact, the children of the guild are just like pupils in a school... with a slight difference : they learn to kill, not to solve mathematical equations. This form of recruitment ensures an homogeneity among members' philosophies and way of killing because they all received the same education, from the same masters.

major skills

Sharpshooter
Your assassins are virtuosos of archery and long-range murder, able to position themselves on the roof of a house and kill their target with an arrow between the eyes, from the other end of a city.
For them, kill without any physical contact is the most elegant form of art.

Supreme Agility
Your assassins have learned to climb any surface and optimally exploit the flexibility of their body. They can slither in the tightest places with ease and run across a city without being slowed down by any obstacle They also developed a guilty pleasure in jumping from roof to roof to leave a crime scene, after executing their target.

Stealth
Everything your assassins do, they do it extremely stealthily. When they walk, you can hear no sound. When they move, it's just like a ghost was dancing around you. The shadow is their natural environment and it is almost impossible to spot them when it is dark.

minor skills
Deadly Dancer
Your assassins are overtrained martial artists capable of neutralizing almost any kind of opponent with bare hands.
Their movements are like an hypnotizing dance that ends with their fists breaking the bones of their target at an amazing speed.

Seducer
Your assassins are all great seducers, with beautiful faces and bodies, endowed with amazing rhetoric. This skill allows them to blend in almost any social environment to approach their target easily.

Illusionist
Your assassins know minor, but very useful, magic tricks, based on the Illusion school. They can temporarily fool the senses of their target, create visual and auditory hallucinations... The most experimented assassins can even become invisible for dozens of seconds but it takes a lot of years to reach such a level.

Observation/ Déduction
No clue or evidence can escape to your assassins' gaze. By watching an individual for a few seconds, studying his mannerism and his gesture, observing the minute details of his clothes, they can learn approximately everything about him, his life and his past. A simple look at a building allows them to spot dozens of security holes and deduce multiples ways to enter in it.

Telepath
Your assassins learned to communicate through the great psychic flow formed by the minds of the other human beings.
During collective missions, your assassins can use telepathy to talk with their comrades, even at great distances from each others, and perfectly coordinate their moves. By focusing, they can vaguely read the thoughts of their target.

Meeting Points
Nobody can go to your headquarters or, at least, you don't accept any non-member of the guild at its proximity. But it exists some particular places, scattered all over the world, where people can contact you if they want to. It can be stones, erected in circle in the middle of a clearing, or abandoned buildings, lost in the wood, or strange monoliths at the foot of a mountain... Whatever these places are, they are always surrounded by a mystical and strange aura. When someone go to one of this point and wait a little, a member of the guild will come out silently of the shadows, will listen to the client and finally, will disappear before informing his masters of a new contract.

Affordable Price
It is possible to use the services of the guild for a modest price. Even a low-income man, saving a lifetime, could have a rival killed by one of your assassins. Of course, don't expect big contracts. Your main targets would be scums, gang leaders, tradespeople, etc... Not very difficult missions, but numerous.

From the Abyss
Cannot be chosen if you chose Disturbing Stranger as a Contact or Lost in Nature as a Location and Come by yourself as a Contact.
Something woke up, in the depths of the Earth. This thing has no age, and its perversion is not measurable by a human brain. Its nature is so rotten to the core that only beginning to understand the magnitude of its malevolence would lead to pure madness.
This time, your contract wasn't submitted to you by an usual client. It's the Emperor himself that contacted you. The supreme ruler of the Continent, the man who holds seventeen kingdoms. His request is urgent and he also called the ten other most powerful assassins' guilds of the Continent to fulfill it. You have to kill the thing. He don't know how, and in fact, nobody knows how, even the greatest scholars of the Continent. But the thing must die, before it arrives to the surface. You must cooperate with the other guilds in order to find a way to execute the thing. In this task, you will have all the money you want, any price you ask. It is, without a doubt, the most important contract you will ever have in your life and probably the last, because if you fail, then nobody will ever be able to desire someone else's death. Gather your best assassins and wish them good luck. They will need it.
