Darkness
Darkness elementals are thick and fluid like ink. They taint all they touch, sapping the light from it. Naturally, they all have the ability to see even in lightless conditions.
On the other hand, they have trouble seeing on a sunny day. While light elementals are relatively constant, darkness elementals grow quite weaker when exposed to sunlight and become much stronger when in dark conditions. They move quickly and silently in the shade, leaving behind dark footsteps.

◆ Corruption ◆
You can corrupt non-elemental creatures with your darkness, covering their body in a dark layer. They'll look like you with smooth, black skin and beaming white eyes. They'll recoil from light and become addicted to darkness. Only intensive light therapy can remove your mark. Those you corrupt will be very much inclined to obey your requests.

◆ Nightmare
You have nigh-unlimited shapeshifting potential as long as no direct light is hitting you. You can grow claws or become as large as the shadow of a building or send out countless inky tendrils to bring all who oppose you into an eternal black nightma-or just disguise yourself as a lamppost or something... Whatever floats your boat.

Eclipse
You can enter a state where you absorb light instead of being repelled by it. A halo will form around you like the moon during a solar eclipse. In addition to a light immunity, this state exudes a magical aura of serenity, making the nearby environment more quiet, calm, and surreal. You can only sustain this state for a few hours at a time.

◆ Paint It Black
You can spread your inky blackness across any substance, even in broad daylight. With effort, it can be washed away, but it requires more than mere rain, and you can spready it easily enough that it doesn't matter. You can spread your darkness several meters from each footstep or concentrate to devour a large area at once.

Possession
This enchantment gives you a new ability. You'll be able to possess people. This is a sort of long-term contract. You must stay near them, and can retreat inside them for safety. In return, your host is immune to your element, and can even wield minor versions of your power. You can lend them more power if you wish, or even consensually merge for yet more power.

Perfection
Cannot be taken with Sealed Power or Hybrid. I can use this enchantment to refine your elemental bond and give you all of the sub-powers for your element! Most natural elementals can't even use that many different abilities! Your elementalism will be exemplified. Perfection! Homogeneity! Well, you kinda already had those traits, you lucky elemental, you! I'm envious!


Elemental Umbrella
Rather than just stopping rain, this umbrella stops elemental energies. It's impervious to elemental forces. It works as an unstoppable shield that can protect you from your opposing element. It does have a couple weaknesses, however. It doesn't have complete coverage, just being an umbrella. It isn't immune to physical harm but regenerates if damaged.

B-besides, there's a lot we can learn from this! Like... how well humans and elementals can really, um... interact in that kind of way. I have a few hypotheses, and all of them make me question why humans always pair up with other humans... A-Anyway, that's an option... Study
The field of elementalism is a vastly under-researched area of magic. I mean, sure, lots of people try to design hotter fireball spells or ways to summon more pure water, but I'm talking about the fundamentals beneath that. I want to find out more about how the elemental realm works, and how you work. Would you mind letting me study you? For science? Er, magic? Y-you know what you mean! I would teach you magic in exchange, but elementals can't learn magic. Conventional magic requires the ability to weave different energies together, but you can only use one type.
