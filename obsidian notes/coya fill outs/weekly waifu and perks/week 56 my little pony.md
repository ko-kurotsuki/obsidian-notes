Open Up Your Eyes: Suffering a trauma is
never a good thing, but hopefully it can teach you things about the world or yourself. This perk will twist it into a power for you, a weapon to protect yourself with going forward. The more crippling or horrible your suffering, the stronger it will be. Bent, but never broken.
Petrification Crystals: You can conjure
small, crystaline globes in your hand to throw at your enemies. When these delicate objects shatter, they release a green cloud that rapidly transforms those caught in it to statues without technically killing them.
Meta-Magic Manacles: You know better
than most the raw power of magic, & while others are content to either shield them-selves from it or to hide away from it you know how to bring it to heel. You can craft tools that can utterly negate magic they come in contact with or with enough resources & preparation even usurp someone else's magic to do your bidding.

Fluttershy Art By: racoonkun
Flutter Batty: In much the way that "Flutterbat"
substitutes the vampire bat for a vampire fruit bat, this perk allows you to substitute the base animals used in transformations for others & to make the-matically appropriate alterations. You could turn a werewolf into a werebear or a wererabbit for example. Provides a form of vegetarian vampirism.
The Stare: I am sure you are familiar with the dreaded stone gaze of a medusa, but were you aware it can be overcome? This effect, when active, pours all of your scorn & disappointment into your sight. This is essentially the disappointed mom & teacher voice rolled into one package. Make pets fall in line, rampaging children straighten up, & yes even make various gorgons blink first. To be clear, if you lock eyes with something, this perk can nullify their gaze effect on you.
Menagerie Mouth: You are one with the beasts of
the earth & sky. Even if they can't normally speak, you understand their meaning, & they yours. This doesn't make them obey you by any means, but after passing the language barrier it is much easier to negotiate & make friends. Be warned: you may get very popular once it gets out to the woodland creatures that you might be able to help.

Art By: Ivl sentrythe2310
Generosity of Spirit: This doesn't keep tally of things, no
tit for tat. This perk is contagious though, that is what it is. The more giving you are to someone, the more they will give to you & to everyone else in their life. From them it spreads to those they give to as well. This doesn't have to be a monetary thing; giving of one's time, com-passion, & lending aid all count. If you are reckless with this you could even turn cities into communities.
Riches Revealed: While this does nothing to actually give
you access to anything you find, this perk allows you to generate a cone of energy focused on your field of vision. This allows you (but no one else) to see valuables obstructed behind barriers or underground. Kinda like x-ray vision, but attuned to wealth. Particularly gems.
Rare Nightmare: This perk allows you to delve deeply
into people's psyches, drawing on the things they find scary or attractive like a deep well of dark magic. It enhances your illusions, allowing you to tailor them from slightly upsetting or alluring all the way to the very deepest & most primal of terrors or obsessions. It can also fuel other forms of magic. Couture, darling.
