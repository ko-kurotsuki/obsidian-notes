Argonaut: A skill for those who want to be a true hero.
Argonaut activates whenever you move to protect someone else. You are then able to parry and counter any attack, reinforcing the durability of the weapon or shield you wield for this purpose, along with boosting your own sturdiness and speed for the action.
Falna: You may now make a pact with a single god or goddess you have access to in person, imbuing you with a fraction of their power, manifested in a boost to your growth in areas governed by the divinity with whom you are bonded. The god or goddess may empower others they find worthy as well, who may ally themselves with you. Gods and goddesses have rather exacting standards for who will be compatible enough to pact with them, but you won't have that issue.

Charm: This ability is an actively-maintained enchantment, leaving your victim in a fog of delirium and infatuation, and fixated on you until you stop channeling the effect. This is no good for standard, benign seduction; the dangers of trying to use it that way cannot be understated.
Mirror, Mirror, on the Wall: You may enchant a mirror in order to spy on others or places from any distance, resisting any efforts to remain unseen or detect viewers. Only you may activate this mirror, and may switch the mirror you enchant, but may only have one enchanted mirror at a time.
Vana Seith: When you best someone, or someone chooses
this option when bargaining with you, you may take their true name for yourself. They will no longer be able to say the name, or be recognized or remembered as themselves by any others. Everything they owned will be seen as belonging to you, including their place in society, and in their family. This lasts until you choose to return it, or they manage to take their name back by force.
