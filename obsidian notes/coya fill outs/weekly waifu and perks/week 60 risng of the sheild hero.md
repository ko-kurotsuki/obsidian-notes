Strike Hero: An indestructible, handheld melee weapon of your choice is
bound to your soul. You may summon and dismiss it at will, but it may not leave your person. You can merge it with any number of other melee weapons of the same kind by holding them while concentrating for a minute, afterwards changing form between them at will. These alternate forms will eventually upgrade the weapon's base form with special powers and attri-butes specific to them once each by mastering or performing great feats with them. It can also absorb special materials and monster parts to gain addi-tional powers and even special upgrades across forms.

Chariot Hero: You gain a great affinity for commanding beasts of burden. Steeds who bear you, pull your carts, ploughs, or otherwise contribute physical labour at your command will be sure of foot and have tireless endurance and blistering speed. Once you manage to strengthen your bond of trust with them they will obey your commands even before you can voice them; they read your body language so well that it seems like telepathy and know you so well that they can predict what you'd want of them.

Sorcery Hero: An indestructible, magical focus that will empower
any spells you cast while wielding it, such as a wand, staff, fan, or spellbook, is bound to your soul. You may summon or dismiss it at will, but it may not leave your person. You can merge it with any number of other foci of the same kind by holding them while con-centrating for a minute, afterwards changing form between them at will. These alternate forms will eventually upgrade the focus's base form with special powers and attributes specific to them once each by mastering or performing great feats with them. It can also absorb special materials and monster parts to gain additional powers and even special upgrades across forms.

Hunting Hero: An indestructible survival tool is bound to your
soul. You can merge it with any number of other survival tools of any kind, such as a shovel, axe, fishing rod, or even a bow and arrow by holding them while concentrating for a minute, afterwards changing form between them at will. You may summon and dismiss it at will, but it may not leave your person. These alternate forms will eventually upgrade each tool type's base form with special powers and attributes specific to them once each by mastering or performing great feats with them. It can also absorb special materials and monster parts to gain additional powers and even special upgrades across forms. It's impossible to use this tool to hurt any sentient being, just monsters and animals.

Hero's Panoply: You can recreate any weapon you've ever held in your hand, provided you have access
to approximate materials, no matter how powerful or legendary. Such weapons lack any intelligence or living spirit the originals may have had and are fixed in the state they are created in, losing any ability to evolve or grow, but retain all other powers and abilities, though those that consume magic do so much less efficiently. Their appearance is slightly tweaked to put your own spin on them. While on your person, you can transform any such weapon you've made into any other such weapon you've ever made. You can allow others to use one of these weapons, but they are only guaranteed to be attuned for your use and a beneficiary can only use one such weapon at a time.

Missile Hero: An indestructible, handheld missile weapon
of your choice is bound to your soul, along with a con-tainer of ammunition for it. You may summon and dismiss both at will, but neither may leave your person. You can merge them with any number of other weapons or pieces of ammunition of the same kind by holding them while concentrating for a minute, afterwards changing form between them at will. These alternate forms will eventu-ally upgrade the weapon's base form with special powers and attributes specific to them once each by mastering or performing great feats with them. They can also absorb special materials and monster parts to gain additional powers and even special upgrades across forms.

Shield Hero 1m : An indestructible shield is bound to your soul.
You may summon and dismiss it at will, but it may not leave your person. You can merge it with any number of other shields by holding them while concentrating for a minute, afterwards changing form between them at will. These alternate forms will eventually upgrade the shield's base form with special powers and attributes specific to them once each by mastering or performing great feats with them. It can also absorb special materials and monster parts to gain additional powers and even special upgrades across forms.

Spirit Physiology 1m: You gain the benefits of your soul having a specific
constitution, linking it more closely with your body. This results in your reserves of spiritual energies, such as mana or what have you, greatly amplifying your physical power, speed, durability, and the power of your spells. The bonus is greatest when you are at full capacity, your power boost lessening proportionally with your energy volume, but use of these bonuses without instinctively holding back to what would otherwise be normal levels will drain your reserves, as will getting hit. Mana potions will be even more overpowered.
