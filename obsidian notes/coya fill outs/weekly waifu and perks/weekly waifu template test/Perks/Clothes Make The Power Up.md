Just changing outfits will now provide you abilities related to the outfits. Putting on boxing gear for example will make you a much better boxer, putting on something with a fiery aesthetic will give you the power of fire, so on and so forth. This also comes with the ability to instantly change clothes just by deciding you want to switch into an outfit. You have an extradimensional pocket capable of storing up to 100 outfits.
