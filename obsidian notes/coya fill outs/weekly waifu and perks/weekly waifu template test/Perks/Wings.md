Pretty simple, you can give a pair of fully functional wings to anybody you want. The wings will be fully capable of carrying their weight and even the weight of another person in the event they need to carry someone, yet also not so big that they're a constant inconvenience. The wings can be dismissed as needed, but you'll have to regrant them to get them back.
