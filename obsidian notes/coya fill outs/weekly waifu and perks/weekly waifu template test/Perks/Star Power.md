An otherwise unobtainable form of magic, this magecraft grows with the amount of power ambient in the world. Should you be in a magic rich world, the very heavens will bend to your will, granting you power to rival or surpass even the strongest of gods. Meteor strikes on command, summoning miniature black holes, laser barrages that mimic a tiny meteor shower and shields of starlight...this power is versatile and dangerous even at its absolute weakest.
