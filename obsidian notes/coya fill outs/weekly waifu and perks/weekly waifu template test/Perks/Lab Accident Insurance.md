Every year, hundreds (well, a bunch anyway) of comic book citizens get caught up in freak accidents that turn them into wacky themed monsters and villains for superheroes to battle. With this perk, you can give them a helping hand, reversing damage to their bodies and minds and quelling unnatural hungers to let them rejoin society as productive members, or at least as less-dangerous criminals. They get to keep any side effects they actually like, of course, though take care; bad people sometimes like the bad parts of what they've become, and they don't get to keep anything.
