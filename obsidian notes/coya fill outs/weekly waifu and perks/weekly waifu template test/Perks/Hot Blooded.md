You can create and control fire and heat from your body, and even use it for all sorts of creative things like making heat illusions or flight! Take care though; you do still need to breathe all that oxygen you're burning up, and while your own fire can't hurt you, it doesn't make you completely fireproof. Optionally comes with free bright red hair, if you're into that.
