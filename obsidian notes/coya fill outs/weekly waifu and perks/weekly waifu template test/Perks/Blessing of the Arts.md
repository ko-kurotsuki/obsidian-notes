If it can be considered an art, you have a talent for it that borders on and sometimes just plain IS supernatural. You can't trigger inspiration for money, but if you go to make something on a whim, it will be by the standards of all who look upon it barring the very most supernaturally skilled of artisans, an utter masterpiece, and even those who could do better will find themselves forced to admit you did a good (if amateurish should you have no prior skill for that thing) job.
