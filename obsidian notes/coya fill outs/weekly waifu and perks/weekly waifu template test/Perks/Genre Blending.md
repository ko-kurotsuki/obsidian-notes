Whether you want more fantasy in your science fiction or more crime drama in your romantic comedy, with this perk, you can mix and match to your heart's content! And success in one area will lead to success in others; a brilliant scientist would find their skills applying equally well to trying to ask out the partner of their dreams and a master swordsman will be able to make his way through a classic "high noon" western.
