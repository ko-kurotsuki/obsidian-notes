This grants an aura of semi-divine energy which will force back both incidental misfortune and malicious curses, as well as being highly detrimental to malicious beings who seek to exploit or harm. Also provides a nice boost to mental fortitude and guarantees a full recovery from any sort of trauma, mental or physical.
