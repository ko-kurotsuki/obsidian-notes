Those you consider your friends, even if they are now officially your enemies, will still go out of their way to help you if you need it. Your friends will also protect your waifus without hesitation in the event they need help even if they do not realize your waifu is your waifu. If you treat them poorly this protection is voided and they'll do what they will.
