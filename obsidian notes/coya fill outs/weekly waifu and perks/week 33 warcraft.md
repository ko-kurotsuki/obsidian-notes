Jaina Proudmoore 3
Game: Warcraft III
Art By: TigrSasha
Arcane Order: You are now a mage of great power and
knowledge. You gain the ability to impose your will upon the invisible network of ley energies coursing through the world, and order them through expenditures of your mana as magical spells. The average mage can channel the elements of arcane energy directly, excite them into flame, or sequence them into frost, attuning you to all three elements. This comes the ability to carry any and all of your magical abilities to any world you visit.
Bind Elemental: You gain the ability to bind an elemental to
your service, and summon it to your side effortlessly. The types of elemental you may subjugate depends on which elements you already have affinity for, and your power level compared to that of your would-be servant. They may instead choose to answer your call and serve you willingly without enslavement, but elementals normally consider people to be unforgivably out of balance with nature and respond to them with murderous violence, so good luck with that.
Brilliance Aura: Not only are you yourself now a brilliant
and creative thinker, your intellect is contagious! Just being around you is enough to inspire your peers to ideas, levels of focus, and mental achievements they never would have been capable of without your radiance, lingering well past your departure.

Sylvannas Windrunner 3
Game: Warcraft III
Art By: Zumi Draws
Black Arrow: Empower your attacks to burn your foes with
necrotic energy. Anyone killed by such an effect will imme-diately raise as your undead minion. Gives you an affinity for death magic, and a talent for empowering yourself through it, including a set of death-magic runes that you may inscribe to release related effects.
Ranger General: You gain inhuman skill with drawn ranged
weapons. You reload faster than the eye can track, can shoot multiple projectiles to hit multiple targets with a single shot when that shouldn't be possible by any logic, and can produce a wide array of trick ammunition that can apply a variety of lesser status effects. These special skills are intended for archers, but a sufficiently skilled gunman can apply them to similar effect as well.
Banshee's Shroud: You can release a dense cloud of inky,
thick, black mist, billowing and blanketing the surrounding area. You gain full spatial awareness of everything within the mist, and may disperse into and out of it at will, only forced into becoming vulnerable if it is blown away. Living opponents that you choose to fully envelop will find their eyes and lungs in great distress. The undead are bolstered while enshrouded.

Alexstrasza 1
Game: Warcraft II
Art By: Felox08

Love For All Creation 1: Humility doesn't have any obvious
practical value, but the villains who always get stomped at the end of the story never seem to practice it. Even someone as powerful as a dragon-god sees the value in compassion and love. With this perk, no matter how far above humanity you ascend, you will never lose your virtues that spring from empathy for others.

Arcane Torrent 1: You can drain energy from outside sources. Drain it from living beings to replenish your reserves, or from natural intersections in ley lines or other fonts of abundant power to amplify your power well past its normal limits.
