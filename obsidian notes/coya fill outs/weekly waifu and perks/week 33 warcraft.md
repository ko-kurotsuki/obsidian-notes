Jaina Proudmoore 3
Game: Warcraft III
Art By: TigrSasha
Arcane Order: You are now a mage of great power and
knowledge. You gain the ability to impose your will upon the invisible network of ley energies coursing through the world, and order them through expenditures of your mana as magical spells. The average mage can channel the elements of arcane energy directly, excite them into flame, or sequence them into frost, attuning you to all three elements. This comes the ability to carry any and all of your magical abilities to any world you visit.
Bind Elemental: You gain the ability to bind an elemental to
your service, and summon it to your side effortlessly. The types of elemental you may subjugate depends on which elements you already have affinity for, and your power level compared to that of your would-be servant. They may instead choose to answer your call and serve you willingly without enslavement, but elementals normally consider people to be unforgivably out of balance with nature and respond to them with murderous violence, so good luck with that.
Brilliance Aura: Not only are you yourself now a brilliant
and creative thinker, your intellect is contagious! Just being around you is enough to inspire your peers to ideas, levels of focus, and mental achievements they never would have been capable of without your radiance, lingering well past your departure.
