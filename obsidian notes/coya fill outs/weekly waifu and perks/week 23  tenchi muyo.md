Series: Tenchi Muyo!
Art By: sugishin46
Dimensional Folding Trick: You know how to make things
bigger on the inside. You can set up a room, a box, all sorts of things so long as they represent a bounded area with firm and opaque borders, and let people and objects freely enter and exit a space inside much bigger (or, if you need to for some reason, smaller) than that boundary would suggest. It can also link multiple points in space and time together, though you need to actually visit one first.
Cosmic Genius: You gain an incredible ability to invent
devices that can do nearly anything, although putting together the resources to actually build them is a different matter. And your understanding in nearly any field of science is virtually unparalleled, though it is intuitive and difficult to explain to others without a sufficient frame of reference. Don't expect to share, is my point. This is a perk for lonely geniuses, misunderstood by and alienated from the world around them... try not to go totally nuts.
Designer Babies: Don't want to experience or put a
partner through all the pain and suffering of childbirth? Or, you know, just not feeling like a romantic relation-ship but still want to experience the joy of parenthood? No problem! With this perk, you can use your own cells, or combine those of yourself and a partner, to produce offspring in a safe, clean environment. They may also get some extra powers if you mix in a few bits of inanimate matter or other tinkering.

Sasami Jurai / Tsunami 3
Series: Tenchi Muyo!
Art By: 286c
Reliable: An understated virtue is just being someone who can
be relied upon to get things done. Well, no longer! So long as you're willing to put in the effort, people will never overlook your contributions just because they aren't flashy or you don't brag and call attention to yourself. And, as a side benefit, you'll pay this forward to those around you and never lose sight of the ways they're being helpful, no matter how humble.
Ship of Theseus: Sometimes, two people meet who are just so
similar, they're like two peas in a pod. With this perk, you can literally merge them into one being. This can only be done will-ingly, and only when there is some similarity between them already, but the combination will be a totally harmonious one, and the final person that emerges will be more complete than either individual. This process will stabilize or remove any injuries or health problems either party has, and thankfully avoid any crushing existential angst.
Wives Like Sisters: The love your partners have for one another
will be just as strong as their love for you. It will take many forms according to their many personalities and how they interact with each other, but sisterly affection will reign. If you like, this love can also become less platonic and more romantic, again, accord-ing to personality, though some may prefer monogamy or tight-knit groups rather than general free love.
