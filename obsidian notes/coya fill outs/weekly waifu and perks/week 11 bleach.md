Yoruichi Shihoin 3
Origin: Bleach
Art By: castell
Flash Master: If you've watched anime for any length of
time, you've probably seen the flash step in action. You put a bit of power (magic, spirit energy, good ol' muscle) into your feet, push off, and move to a point you can see so fast you seem to be teleporting. With this perk you can not only do it but master it, out-speeding even other speed demons and reacting faster than the eye can follow, and the distance you can cover with each flash step is truly immense.
Kido and Hado: You've mastered ninety-nine spells of
binding and destruction, respectively, with this perk. These can achieve a wide variety of potent and useful effects, from instantly putting an opponent in a super-natural armlock at the low end to summoning a bunch of giant energy dragons on the other. The spells can only be resisted or broken by sufficient spiritual power and can be made stronger with proper incantations, though using them without them can still be plenty effective. Normally each one needs practice; congrats on getting them all for free!
Little Bit Beastly: When you get this perk, pick a reason-
ably-sized animal. You can turn into that animal at will. You can remain transformed indefinitely and still talk in a completely different voice, so it's actually potentially pretty good for disguising yourself, lying low, and sneaking around; nothing can reveal you're anything but that animal. You can also instead use the perk to trans-form your mind, becoming feral and beastial in thought but gaining great powers related to the animal you picked. Free advice, maybe don't stay this way too long...

I Reject! 1: You gain the power to affect the physical world
through rejection, preventing attacks, reversing injury, or even tearing apart enemies in direct proportion to how deeply you refuse to accept the outcome you're preventing. However, it is deeply tied to your personality; a nurturing type might be able to reverse even death but struggle to work up the outrage to really hurt someone, while an angrier person might find it easy to lash out but lack the fear to block.
