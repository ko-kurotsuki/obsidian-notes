Serena / Usagi Tsukino "Sailor Moon"
Series: Sailor Moon
Art By: Axsens & Saotome Nanda
In the Name of the Moon!: You gain a small, ornate item that can be easily hidden on your person. When you lift it high and invoke its power, whatever you're wearing will temporarily transform into a spiffy suit of supremely damage resistant clothes balancing sexy and fashionable. They grant you many powers, including strong protection from a wide array of threats, the ability to purify evil forces and influences through flashy violence, and powerful identity concealment, regardless of actual disguise.
Utopia: Does it mean "good place" or "no place?" Regard-less, this perk will guide you towards actions that will ulti-mately create an ideal form of government that does right by all its people. It may not be perfect, and it definitely won't be easy, but to the extent to which such a thing is possible, it will be achievable, if you have the courage and strength to strive for it.
A Good Cry: That's it, let it all out. If ever you find yourself
under too much pressure and stress to take, taking a moment to turn into a blubbering heap will refresh you, physically, mentally, and spiritually, leaving you better equipped to deal with the problem that left you in such a mess to begin with. The more catastrophic and intense your emotional release, the more powerful and effective this becomes.

"Sailor Mercury"
Series: Sailor Moon

The Dreaded A Minus! 1m: This perk will put a "floor" under your per-formance in proportion to your aptitude for it and the time and energy you usually put into it. For example, If you're usually a straight-A student who spends most of your week studying, you'll never do worse than an A- even if you make a catastrophic mistake, while if you're usually average then a failing grade might barely pass.

Rini/Usagi Serenity
"Chibi-Usa" / "Wicked/Black Lady"
Series: Sailor Moon
Art By: psychlopath & Sakimichan
Malefic Black Crystal 1m: You gain a chunk of midnight-colored crystal, which channels the raw power of death,
negativity and darkness. It grows in size and potency the more of these things it is exposed to, although the chan-neller is somewhat resistant to its effects. Portions of it can be broken off and formed into jewelry, which can feed the original and distort the memories of the wearer, filling their mind with negative thoughts. Potentially world-threatening if brought to fruition, but try not to expose it to too much true love or genuine happiness; acts of either cause it to shrink, or even shatter if dramatic enough!
Temporal Reinforcement 1m: You see it all the time in movies:
someone mucks around in time, and then suddenly they or someone they care about accidentally gets erased from history. This perk represents some kind of insurance for that. You might want to, you know, try to go back and resolve the paradox you represent at *some* point, but tampering with time will never make you cease to exist.

Queen Beryl
Series: Sailor Moon
Art By: Helltzujin
Antagonist Adjuster 1m: One interesting facet of visiting
other worlds is seeing how the antagonistic inhabit-ants of those worlds can do similar things despite being very different people. With this perk, you can make minor adjustments to the "villainous" inhabit-ants of worlds you have access to. Although you can't change their actions with this alone, you can control their motivations, adjusting evil fanaticism or selfish-ness into tragic desperation or vice-versa, and there-fore how recepitive they might or might not be to changing their ways or fighting on to the bitter end.
