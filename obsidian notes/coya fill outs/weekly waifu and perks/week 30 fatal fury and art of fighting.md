Mai Shiranui 3
Game: Fatal Fury II
Art By: Zesker &
Flowerxl

Elemental Channel: You can channel a particular element
into your physical strikes and anything you touch, even potentially wreathing your body in it for brief moments. While your own powers can't hurt you, this ability grants no special resistance against that element in general. However, such channelling can always overpower the special resistances of others, giving you a bit of an edge.

Widow Woman: Because sometimes, the sadder but wiser girl's the girl for you. With this perk, there's something about you that draws the attention of people who've been around the block and felt the pain of loss. So long as you try to help them, reach out to them, form an emotional connection to them, they'll find it easier to open up, move on, and find new happiness instead of remaining trapped in the past.

Nice Legs: With this perk, almost anything you can do with your arms and hands can also be done with your legs and feet. From pushing and lifting to shooting energy blasts to opening door-knobs, the possibilities are endless. Also comes with a bit of extra flexibility and balance, so that you can better move your legs around properly.

Yuri Sakazaki 2
Game: Art of Fighting II
Art By: Amania Orz &
RyukiBlade64

The Basics: Flashy elite tricks aren't as important as a
good solid foundation of fundamentals! So long as you master the basic elements of something, from cooking to kung fu, you'll be able to hold your own against masters. Maybe not surpass them immediately, but any further advanced techniques will be something you can sous out on your own, even coming up with your own new ideas, just by following the logic built in to the foundations.

Sinclair Game: Art of Fighting III 3
Art By: Unknown
Dancing Sword: Whenever you let go of a weapon
you're holding, you can continue to control it with your mind, causing it to hover in thin air and move around to strike as hard as it would if you were actually holding it. This is about as mentally-taxing as actually fighting with such a weapon, but it's rela-tively easy to return it back to your hand and catch it. (You can use this on ranged weapons, but not the projectiles they shoot.)
Winners Don't Use Drugs: Foreign substances have no
power over you. So long as you didn't willingly imbibe it, a drug won't affect your body or mind, and such things will quickly and harmlessly flush out of your system. It won't work on things you willingly put into your body, but this perk will also help you quickly shed any addictions you already have.
Aura of Mystery: Whenever you like, you can cloak
your actions in enigma, making your true motivations and designs very difficult to discern or investigate. This can eventually be overcome with sufficient evidence, and actually being around you offers some resistance, but even those you're planning to betray will struggle to see it coming until it's too late! Also, being mysterious and intriguing offers more than a few social benefits, so long as you're able to help make sure the pay-off is worth the wait for the people around you...
