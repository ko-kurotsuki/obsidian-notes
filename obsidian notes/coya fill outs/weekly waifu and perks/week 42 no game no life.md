Aschente: Wanna play a game? If you &
another person agree to a bet, this perk allows for the magical exchange of anything owned by the respective parties, even intangible things like memories, life force, powers, etc. Facili-tates and enforces the bets, no more relying on handshakes & promises.
Needle In A Haystack: You've uncanny
luck when it comes to finding what you need. If impossible to find currently, either you will find a world where it is, or fate will make sure it finds its way to you. You'll know it when you see it.
Thanks For All The Fish: Godly beings find it is all too easy to forget the value of a shared meal & good conversation. Any meal you share will have riveting con-versation & be enriching for all involved. There is always more to learn. The true enemy of eternity is boredom, ennui be damned.

Fiel Nirvalen Official Art
Hexa-caster: Strictly speaking multitasking
isn't really a thing, it is just changing between tasks quickly doing more tasks at a lower quality. Not for you though. You can flawlessly concentrate on up to six things without any loss of performance. May be used to concen-trate on multiple spells.
Infectious Loyalty: You have a special type of
usurpation magic. Animated objects will obey your commands. So will any constructs they come in contact with. Valid targets include but are not limited to golems, robots, computers, marionets, clockwork automata, etc. Mystical computer virus, anyone?
Think and Fiel: With guts, pluck, and a whole
lot of trial and error, you can try to tinker with various magical forces to create new spells and effects. As a side benefit, you can always use magic, even if you couldn't before or are in a world where magic normally wouldn't exist!

Jibril Art By: Hong-BsWs
A Head For It: When the angels could no longer collect their enemies' heads they started col-lecting their knowledge. Now you can too. When dealing what would be a lethal blow you can, instead, learn everything they know. Why let secrets die? Knowledge gained is forfeited if your target dies by your hand within 24 hours.
Exceptional Unit: You have the Gift of Imperfec-
tion. The flaws in yourself & others are opportu-nities. This allows you to easily identify & capitalize on them. Non-standard insights, novel approaches, there are always ways to improve yourself & the things around you.
Defense Magic: Protection against all kinds of
threats, from eavesdropping to atomic bombs. Mastery over shields, wards, & other protective effects. This also augments & reinforces other magics you cast. Casting time may vary.
