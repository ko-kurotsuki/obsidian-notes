Aqua 3
Origin: Konosuba
Art By: Hormone Kojirou
Summon Water: You can, as needed, summon as much
water as you want. Fill a swimming pool, extinguish fires, have a drink in the desert, drown your enemies in a mighty tide of the goddess's wrath...It's actually surprisingly useful!
Blessing of the Arts: If it can be considered an art, you
have a talent for it that borders on and sometimes just plain IS supernatural. You can't trigger inspiration for money, but if you go to make something on a whim, it will be by the standards of all who look upon it barring the very most supernatually skilled of artisans, an utter masterpiece, and even those who could do better will find themselves forced to admit you did a good (if ama-teurish should you have no prior skill for that thing) job.
Purification: Any liquid you touch, you can purify into the
cleanest and purest water. The larger a body of water is, the longer it will take to purify it, but as a nice bonus, any water you purify will become potent holy water with strong healing properties for both the body and soul, and it's also like acid to demons and the undead.

Darkness 3
Origin: Konosuba
Art By: Ataruman
Masochist EX: Simply put, pain is pleasure. Humiliation is
now pure ecstasy. Violation and degredation, even just outright molestation, mistreatment and abuse are now truly exciting and outright fun. This can be shared with anyone you choose, and turned on or off as desired.
WARNING: JUST BECAUSE THIS MAKES PAIN FUN IT DOES NOT MEAN YOU ARE NOT STILL POSSIBLY GETTING HURT!
Sensitive Steel: Your body becomes as strong and hard as
steel but does not lose any of your sensitivity to prevent loss of dexterity and pleasure. Does stack multiplicatively with any other durability enhancing effects your body may be under. This is automatically shared with anyone under the effects of Masochist EX.
Misinterpreted In The Best Possible Way: No matter how selfish or absurd your actual motivations may be, any action you take will be interpreted in the best way possible for you by any bystanders who don't know you or your motivations well enough to guess what's going on in your head at the time. Your friends won't be fooled, but anyone else will see whatever you're doing in the best light possible. Won't let you get away with crimes, but any sort of wacky shenanigans that could be logically seen in a good light will be, even if that logic is completely wrong...

Megumin 3
Origin: Konosuba
Art By: Hormone Kojirou
KABOOM MOTHERFUCKERS!: Once a day, you can turn all of
your magical energy for the day into a truly super massive EXPLOOOOOO000000000SION!!! which is powerful enough to wipe out...basically anything your abilities would normally be able to over the course of a full day of constant full throttle attack. Your mileage will vary based on your power levels. A normy might only be able to make the equivalent of a grenade, a good wizard would equal out to a small nuke.
Best Pick: When you are required to select a single option from several choices, you will find in the long run that the one you selected is in fact the best for you. In short, you will never experience regrets over the choices you have made, very useful to anyone with an extended lifespan.
Loophole Abuse: An entire TEAM of lawyers could not
prevent you from finding some absurd loophole in any agreement or the rules of any competition you may find yourself entering into. These loopholes will always turn whatever your situation is to your distinct and often flat out overwhelming advantage. Do be wary that this only really helps when the other person is willing to play by the rules.
