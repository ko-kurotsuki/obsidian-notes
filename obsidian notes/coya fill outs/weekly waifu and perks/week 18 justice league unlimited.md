Galatea
Who Says A Copy Can't Surpass The Original? :
Any ability or weapon you use which could be considered a copy can and will equal the original, and possibly even surpass it.
Nothing Like A Good Brawl: Even if you don't
normally enjoying fighting, you can now choose to make it an exhilirating experience that will guarantee your enjoyment so long as you're not on the receiving end of a one sided beatdown.
Mental Shielding: What's more frustrating than
somebody messing with your head? Well no matter your answer, you'll never have to worry about that again (people messing with your head that is). Your mind can no longer be read, nor can any sort of psychic ability influence your mind. Your mind is yours and yours alone!

HAWKGIRL
Anti-Magic: You can imbue a weapon with
anti-magic which will pierce straight through magic defenses, negate offenive magic if used to block, and deal damage that can't be healed by magic.
Wings: Pretty simple, you can give a pair of fully functional wings to anybody you want.
The wings will be fully capable of Carrying their weight and even the weight of another person in the event they need to carry someone, yet alsO not so big that they're a constant inconvenience. The wings can be dismissed as needed, but you'll have to regrant them to get them back.
Party HARD: Sometimes a regular party just isn't
enough fun. What it needs is some action! Devolve any party into a full on brawl that won't see anyone severely injured even if everyone involved goes full throttle and leave everyone satisfied, laughing and too tired to move in the aftermath.

WONDER WOMAN
I Want The Truth!: By focusing on someone, you
Can force them to tell the truth, the whole truth and nothing but the truth in response to anything they are asked.
Divine Allies: You will quickly gain the attention
of any divinities in any world you visit and usually be able to make friends with them. This may result in quests, mayhem, or demigods.
Amazon Land: You can designate a space of land
to become Amazon land, which will be populated entirely by badass women who also happen to be extremely attractive. They will welcome women with open arms, but whether they welcome men with open fire or open legs is entirely up to you.
(Yes, you can make it so they only welcome certain men in one way or the other)
