Maki Oze
Series: Fire Force
Art By: Cutesexyrobutts
Second Generation 1: A less resistant adaptation
than Third Generation this still boosts strength, durability, & resistance to heat. However, this also grants pyrokenesis allowing you to manipulate & control existing flames. Having multiple Generation abilities causes them to synergize, becoming expo-nentially more powerful & displaying physics-defying power.
Bewitching 1m: You can tame the elements, breath-
ing sentience in to them. Your sprites are physi-cally strong enough to lift and wield armor or weapons to devastating effect. You can merge multiple sprites of compatible elements into large elementals. It is good to make friends.
Ogre Princess 1m: A pretty face is too often dismissed
as being nothing more than eye candy. Rippling muscles cause people to forget about a rapier wit. Being a threat in unexpected ways is quite a boon. This allows you to gauge expectations & determine what about you others would be most cautious. Knowing this, you can see what would be the most effective form of misdirection to use.


Sister Iris

Doppelgänger: Everyone exists in reality, but
there is another version of them in the minds of those who know them. From the collective unconscious you can manifest & banish someone that is a reflection of what people think of you. This can be better or worse than you really are. Manage your PR.

Arrow & Haumea
Series: Fire Force
Art By: DyuN
Radiohead 1: You gain the ability to remotely
generate and perceive electricity at close to long ranges. Even with no finesse you can easily elec-trocute people and fry electronics. With restraint you can remotely bridge circuits, turning on devices. With enough mastery & control one can even emulate telepathy, generating & reading electrical activity in brains.
Motörhead 1m: You can stimulate a person's
endorphins & neurotransmitters to scramble behavior. This usually results in heightened aggression & lowered inhibitions. Even as a clumsy manipulation the chaos can be quite disruptive. Particularly effective against animals, infernals, & demons.
Cannon Fire 1m : This perk enhances eyesight so
you can pinpoint extremely long-range targets with the precision of a sniper. This telescopic sight allows you to augment all variety of shots fired to effectively be artillary. Also provides a small suite of skills: taking in to account moving targets, wind resistance, even the curvature of whatever planet you are on and the local constant of gravity. Fiery shots may even be manipulated so long as you can see them, guiding them toward targets.
