Buzam A. Calessa "Tenmei Uragasumi"
Series: Vandread
Art By: Kuroda Kazuya
Transitory Attraction: Contrary to the name, this perk will
instead help cement ties and bonds. If the things that first attracted others to you are real and unfeigned, learning other things, even other misapprehensions, won't erode or destroy them. It could be as simple as learning you like a movie you don't like or as dramatic as discovering your orientation; what matters is your positive qualities as a person.
Going Native: If you ever wanted to just cut ties with your
old life and society, this is for you! This perk will let you smoothly integrate into another culture, even changing your name and identity if necessary, and if you decide you like it more than your current one, you'll be able to find love and acceptance there without issue.
Fieldcraft: Usually, leading and doing are not necessarily
identical skillsets. Good soldiers aren't always skilled generals, and the talents that make a great spymaster don't automatically make a great spy. This perk blurs that division. You'll gain a boost in skill and aptitude in the other area proportional to your competence in the one, whether it's becoming a great warrior by virtue of being a great tactician or vice versa. This is a bit diluted if you are *already* good at both areas, but you'll still see some improvements anyway.

Gascogne Rhinegau
Series: Vandread
Art By: Unknown (Seriously)
Logistics: Don't knock it. Tactics win battles, but this
wins wars. You've got a knack for keeping track of all your supplies, making sure people get what they need, and figuring out how to move them around without running into all kinds of complications. At the very least, you'll never need to stress about moving house ever again! And this scales up and down as much as you like; you can handle this at both the grand strategy level and when you shop.
Card Shark: You have a strong affinity for games that
combine chance with skill and head games, like mahjong, dominos, and, yes, poker. Whether it's bluffing or folding, you can bring both strong instincts and clever strategy to bear at the table, and you have a good sense for people and their tells that can lead you to victory and success.
Catalyst: Something about you drives others to
better themselves and mature past their flaws. This could take the form of being a good listener who helps others to work through their thoughts and problems, or of just being a good friend who supports them when they need it most. Sometimes, it could even be something that happens when you have to leave their lives for a while, resulting in a big hole that they then have to fill. Either way, people's lives will start to change as a result of meeting you.

Dita Liebely
Series: Vandread
Art By: Higashiyama
Dream Come True: We all have our special interests we've
accepted will probably never happen, right? Now they can. Whether it's actually getting to meet an alien, being able to live on a submarine, or getting to experience some of the best foods in the world, if it's your greatest passion you're guaranteed to have the chance to experience it at least once in your life.
Be Of Good Cheer: You've a knack for keeping other
people's spirits up by maintaining a positive attitude. Notably, you yourself don't necessarily *have* to be happy on the inside; so long as you fake it, those around you will perk up and never fall into despair. And after all, some-times all it takes to feel better yourself is to see others around you feeling good, right?
Combine: Normally, the mechanical difficulties of getting
two machines to merge and share systems are staggering. This perk elides a bit of that. With it, you can easily combine and mix-and-match parts from a broad variety of devices, even computers. It works best when these machines share a function, but you could even use a car for a cockpit in a pinch! And machines of the same kind will increase in power and effectiveness as they merge together too.
