Moka Ayashiya
(Inner, Outer, and a Shuzen Family Value Pack!)
Art By: Rankerhen & dieselturbo Series: Rosario + Vampire
Know Your Place!: Contempt is your armor and pride
is your sword! With this perk, you gain strength and potency while battling against those you feel to be overstepping their bounds, especially if they're also your social inferiors, intimidating them in the process. Works regardless of whether you're exercis-ing noblesse oblige or just being kind of a classist and elitist jerk about things.
Bathtub Herbs: You have a means of preventing one
of your greatest weaknesses from destroying you. Whether it's as simple as food that gives you indiges-tion or triggers allergies, or as serious as some genuine supernatural anathema that could cripple your powers or even kill you, you know a way to nullify it and let you enjoy a given situation with a bit of prep-work. It doesn't work so great on "attacks" though.
One Big Happy(?) Family: We don't all have to get
along or agree, but we can all at least admit we're family, right? With this perk, you and your extended family can at least tolerate each other and get along on holidays and family reunions. It won't suddenly make everyone friends or stop any genuine differ-ences of opinion or ideology from being a thing, but it will at least ensure nasty arguments or worse never escalate into anything toxic, and diametrically opposed viewpoints will at least find tolerance.

Mizore Shirayuki
Series: Rosario + Vampire
Art By: Blue-senpai
Biological Clockstopper: Worried about running out of time
to have kids? No worries. With this perk, a partner's body will never lose its fertility unless they let it. Just... maybe don't wait until they're too old? The perk will provide some protection, but pregnancy is still hard on the body. It also provides similar benefits for any other abilities they might have that would diminish with age.
People Watching: Don't call it stalking, okay! It's completely
different! With this perk, you can follow people around mostly-unnoticed, even instinctively sliding into places others wouldn't expect you to go, to spy on them and try to learn about what they're doing. If or when they do find out, this perk will also keep them from being too creeped out by the whole thing, though it will still be around the "harmless eccentricity" level of weirdness.
Harmless Freezing: Your powers always come with a built-in
safety switch. No matter how incredibly deadly they'd logi-cally seem, you can always choose to pull your punch, and just make them painful but debilitating if you want.

Kurumu Kurono
Series: Rosario + Vampire
Art By: CreatureCola
A Kiss Between Friends: Are you or someone you know just
feeling down? With this perk, just give them a smooch to brighten up their day, or even get them to open up and talk about what's wrong! Works with all sorts of kisses, although the more... passionate, the more powerful.
Dream Weaver: You, and those you bring with you, can enter
a nearby person's dreams and get a window into their sub-conscious. Everyone will be protected against anything dan-gerous or bad that they might be dreaming of, but for the most part stuff can't be taken out of the dream with you, with the very important exception of children conceived in the dream.
Field of Dreams: You can conjure illusions into the waking
world. They are incredibly convincing, able to cheat all senses, and sufficiently powerful ones can even cause per-manent harm or kill, although this power is fueled by the strength of your emotions and stronger illusions are more effective according to your own emotional state.
