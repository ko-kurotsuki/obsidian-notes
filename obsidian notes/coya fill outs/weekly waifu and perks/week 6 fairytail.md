Irene Belserion 3
Origin: Fairy Tail
Art By: doha skylightscent
Polymorph: Change the shape of yourself... or basically
anything else. Turn somebody into a frog? No problem. Turn yourself into a dragon? No prob. Reshape an entire country to your liking? Tricky, but not impossible if you've got the power on tap for it.
Create Minion: A useful ability, you can enchant per-
sonalities onto objects and grant them life. This can be as simple as animating a statue to make a golem...or turning a sword into a cute girl with magical abilities and personality of your own choosing. The enchant-ments can be broken with enough damage, but they can prove remarkably resilient.
Fight Fire With Fire: If you can obtain a sample of a
certain type of power, you can grant people the ability to replicate it as naturally as if it were their own. Things like using a bit of a dragon's magic to grant a human power identical to that dragon, although this is not limited to dragons. Gods, demons, unicorns, whatever. You sample it, you can imbue it and fling it right back in their face.

Erza Scarlet 3
Origin: Fairy Tail
Art By: user cxka7875 & shexyo
Clothes Make The Power Up: Just changing outfits
will now provide you abilities related to the outifits. Putting on boxing gear for example will make you a much better boxer, putting on something with a fiery aesthetic will give you the power of fire, so on and so forth. This also comes with the ability to instantly change clothes just by deciding you want to switch into an outfit. You have an extradimensional pocket capable of storing up to 100 outfits.
Power of Friendship: As long as you're doing some-
thing for the sake of someone you care about, you will be able to pull it off, no matter how ludicrously impossible it may appear to be at a glance.
Royal Bearing: Gain an aura of command and author-
ity that will make everyone want to shut the hell up and listen whenever you get serious. Also allows you to mete out punishment to those who have broken the rules as you see fit, and grants you a bit of leeway to act a bit kooky as most people are too scared to challenge you.
