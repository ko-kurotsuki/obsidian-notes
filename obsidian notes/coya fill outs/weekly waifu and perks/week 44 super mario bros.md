Supercrown Deluxe: You gain the ability to grant more
feminine and human features to sapient creatures. This can be as extreme of a transformation as you want, from giving legs to a mermaid to completely transforming a male turtle monster into a woman of supermodel-level beauty. They are also granted a small crown which looks like a pink mushroom with golden points to wear if they so choose. If they're dissatisfied with their transformation (the chances of which are statistically insignificant) and want to change back, they can do so by willingly discarding the crown or giving it to someone else, who gains the effect instead.
Spook: Similar to this perk is the thusfar-unrevealed
reason why ghosts are so into frightening good folk; when others fear you their natural resistance to your powers and attacks will dwindle proportionately. Even startling someone will linger on in your presence, fear held fast by the power of this perk. Inspiring true terror of you will develop into a certifiable phobia or PTSD. This also gives you the ability to morph and stretch your face into a more frightening expression at will, and your tongue able to extend much, much further.
Bowsette's Fury: You gain the ability to rapidly grow
into a giant, at least the size of a building, throwing you into an uncontrollable rage. Screw the square-cube law, that's for people who aren't you; you're exempt from it. Your features become more intimidating for the duration of the transformation, and all of your abilities become proportionally bigger, and more powerful. What would have been a potentially lethal blow in your normal form might not even be enough to break through your skin. This lasts until you rage yourself out, upon which you'll fall fast asleep.

energy of the stars, and other celestial bodies. You can use this energy interchangeably with your life force, and channel it into simple physical barriers, rings of force and radiation, lessening the effects of gravity to float, levitate, and spin, and survive in the vacuum of space. ings to become invulnerable for a brief period of time. You shine a bright, iridescent gold and sparkle for the duration. Certain items might give you an additional activation or an additional activation per day, if you can strike on one. If you're crushed in a cave-in, drowning in molten lava, collaps-ing under great underwater pressures, etc., you're going to have to escape before the duration runs out.

is great for friendships, and when you organize a sporting event, all invitees will be likely to find themselves in favour-able circumstances to attend, and all of you will gain at least a competent level of skill in the event's game or sport, perma-nently.
