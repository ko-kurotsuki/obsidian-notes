Kaguya Ootsutsuki 3
Origin: Naruto Shippuden
Art By: Supullium
The Source: You gain the ability to replicate any
power which is derived from a bloodline or can be passed down through one. Your replicated ability will start out basic, but you can improve it to equal and even greatly surpass the original.

Powerhouse: Chi, chakra, mana, whatever you want
to call it, whatever the power system of the world you visit runs on, if it is reliant upon internally con-tained personal reservoirs of that power, your reser-voir is now in no uncertain terms that of a TITAN. Your reserves will be equal to that of every other being capable of calling on this power system within a planet width radius of you, thus only absurd waste will even begin to strain your reserves, and feats requiring otherwise prohibitive amounts of power will be child's play for you.

Variations: You gain the ability to jump between dif-
ferent versions of the worlds you've chosen, 'canon', 'non-canon', 'fan fiction', even so called 'abridged' variations of your world of choice are now available to you to visit.

Naruko Uzumaki 3
Origin: Naruto
Art By: KC
Shadow Clones: Summon up to a hundred clones of
yourself, which while capable of physically interacting with the world will become more fragile with every clone you make to the point where at a hundred clones a good shove is enough to disperse one. You receive the memories of the clones when they're dispersed, but the clones can do anything you can as long as they're around.

Transformation: Look however you want, whenever you
want. This also affects your voice and is a physical trans-formation. You can change back or into a new form whenever you want.

Endless Stamina: No more getting tired for you! You are a
seemingly endless well of physical energy. Don't try staying up for a month straight of constant hard exercise, but nothing much less than that will be able to seriously tire you out.
