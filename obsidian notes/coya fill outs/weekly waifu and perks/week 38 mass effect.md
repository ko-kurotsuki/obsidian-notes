Delayed Gratification: Sometimes, the sweetest fruit is the

several nodes of a strange substance that defies many of the normal laws of physics when electricity passes through it... electricity like the stuff crackling in your brain right now as you read this. Upon properly mastering this ability, you can alter force and mass for yourself and for everything around you, which has all sorts of strange applications. Unfortunately, this power is not the easiest thing in the world to learn and master, but by taking this perk you'll get a head start and find it comes very natu-rally to you, and ceiling on the potential amount of manip-ulation you'll be able to achieve is enormous.

Gel Packs and Cushions: Lots of sculptures and constructs
are generally less soft and inviting then they appear. This perk allows them to instead offer exactly the same level of comfort they normally would, like a statue of a furry animal that feels just as fluffy to the touch as the real thing. As a good rule of thumb, the more detailed the simulacra, the more powerful the effect, although autom-atons and other moving devices are generally the most strongly affected.
Somebody Plugged In the Overlord: You can put your mind into a sufficiently advanced machine or construct, allowing you to exist as pure thought. You still get to keep your body while you do it, although multitasking skills are recommended, and can even transfer your mind into another body given time and a sufficiently advanced body. And while it's inside the machine, your mind can control it and other machines affected by it, something that's most useful if the machine in question is some kind of computer attached to a building or starship.
Machine Charisma: You have a natural knack for socially
interacting with the alien minds of artificial creatures, like robots, artificial intelligences, golems, and so on. Self-actualizing is easier for them with your help.

Paragon/Renegade: You'll find that you can get strong
results from either adopting a calm, kind, open-minded and non-judgemental yet uncompromisingly moral position or a brash, selfish, take-no-prisoners loose cannon style that doesn't play by the rules but gets results will become more effective the more you lean into it, allowing you to more easily persuade those around you to listen to your ideas or adopt your point of view while boosting your personal will-power. Strangely, nothing stops you from alternating between the two, almost as if your attunement to either perspective was being tracked separately, despite their diametric differences.
And This Is My Favorite Store On the Citadel: So long as you're sufficiently famous, you'll find that people are more willing to cut business deals with you or give you a discount so long as you're willing to give them a quick endorsement. The more famous you are, the better the deal in question, and this specifically scales very strongly with both attunements if you also took Paragon/Renegade.
Quick-Time Interrupt: You have a sixth sense for the
best moment to cut people off, whether in a well-intentioned bid to give them emotional support or cut off a spiral, or to suddenly do something brutal and/or badass they don't see coming. It scales well with general awareness and physical abilities, but isn't as essential as you'd think! And your "prompts," for lack of better terminology, will only trigger when there won't be immediate consequences for acting on them.
