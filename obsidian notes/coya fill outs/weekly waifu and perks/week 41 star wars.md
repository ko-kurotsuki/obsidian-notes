Mara Jade Skywalker Novel: Heir to the Empire
Art By: Patrick Finch
Force Sensitivity: You gain an extra sensory power, making you
aware of an energy field surrounding and enfolding all living things. With this awareness comes the power to manipulate it, simultaneously allowing it to guide your actions while it also obeys your commands. Incredible miracles can be accomplished with this power, from telepathic and kinetic powers to mending or destroying flesh and life to gazing into the future. But take care. This power works best when a strong, healthy mind and soul embraces it in the spirit of humility and enlightenment, and gains a healthy connection to the natural world. Those who instead choose to gratify themselves at the expense of all others will find their heart's desire and find despair along the dark path of spiritual corruption and degedation.
Medicine for the Bitter Heart: Unless you allow it to, the world
will never break you down or cause you to become too bitter and jaded to love life and those in it.
Jack of All Trades: You have a baseline level of competence in
almost every kind of skill you put your hand or mind to. You might not outcompete someone fully trained in a given area, or be able to accomplish the sorts of feats true specialists can, but from computers to vehicles, from medicine to spywork, from all manner of academic knowledge to all manner of combat skills, you will never, ever be caught completely helpless and ignorant in a bad situation ever again!

Alema Rar "Darth Talon" Comic: Star Wars: Legacy
Art By: Nyuunzi
Drain Knowledge: You can dig into the mind of a sapient
creature and seize information, or instead force information into it. This can be done without the creature's consent, which is almost always pretty traumatic and unpleasant, and even with their consent is generally pretty off-putting. You can choose whether or not you copy or totally remove the knowledge in question in the process; the latter is almost invariably much more traumatic than the former, again, especially without consent.
Fanatical Follower: One of the darker powers in our catalogue.
With this, you can mold others into unquestioning obedience over time, willing to do literally anything and everything you demand of them, regardless of their own self-interest or well-being. You can also apply this to yourself, inspiring the same fanatical devotion to a set of ideals rather than a person. Just be careful: brainwashed fanatics are generally less creative and effective than free people freely helping.
Sexy Murderer: Your natural appeal is a weapon. In fights,
enemies will either be distracted by their attraction to or jealousy towards you, according to orientation. Even foes without much sexual inclination will at least appreciate you aesthetically. And yes, this boosts your natural appearance too.

Rey Palpatine/Skywalker Film: Star Wars: The Force Awakens Art By: DevilHS & VirusinfectedArt
Scavenger Punk: You're an expert at sorting through
junk and finding treasure. Whether it's rare and valuable components hidden in a mountain of trash, or stuff others just fail to see the true value in, you can almost always find what you need if you've got the stomach for it. And as a side benefit, it's a lot easier to clean the smell off yourself and scrub the residue off your body when you're done than it other-wise would be.
Prospect Insurance: We all know ideas or people who,
sadly, just never live up to their full potential. Maybe they just make poor choices or double-down on mistakes, maybe they panic and flail around at the first sign of trouble, and maybe they just can't seem to bring themselves to overcome their worst impulses. They are their own worst enemies. Well, with this perk, you can help them, and potentially yourself if you* are your own worst enemy! It will help them avoid these pitfalls, or to dig their way out of any pits they've already fallen in, eventually accomplishing the great things you always knew they (or you) could!
Beginner's Luck: You get a huge boost in capability
whenever you try something out for the very first time. From making a dramatic escape from the detention block of a space station to outgunning trained fighter pilots to firing a torpedo down a two-meter wide exhaust port to getting in a swordfight with experienced opponents, you can always rely on fortune's favor and intuition to carry you through at least once. Just be careful; it fades pretty fast.
