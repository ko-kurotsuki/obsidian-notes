This is one of the single most divine places in the multiverse.
This place can be absolutely filled with Goddesses working at their maximum ability. You barely need to make any choices.

Hierarchy Pantheon
They are still one group, but they are not all equal. The God-desses will be organized into tiers with those of higher tiers having greater power, influence, and worshiped more fervently.
You may decide the number and population of each tier.

Intertwined
Separation is for divorcees. The Goddesses walk among the mortals in this world and take a very direct role in leading the world and their followers' lives. The Holy Realm(s) still exist, but they are places on the planet that even mortals can potentially reach rather than seperate planes.

GODDESS OF ROYALTY
Blessing of Royalty
The people of this world will be naturally inclinded towards heirarchical relationships.
Monarchies and class systems are practically a given to develop. They will feel this on an instinctual level. Some will feel the drive to climb to the ranks, others will be content where the are, and others may even crave to fall further.

Divine Right
Are you worried that corrupt Kings and Queens will bring ruin to the land? The God-desses will be the ultimate check on their power. When a monarch is to take the throne, I (or another Goddess) will choose the person best suited to leading the nation in its current state. You may decide if there are limits, or if literally anyone can be selected.
Alpha & Omega
A biological difference will appear in people living in this world. The more dominant ones will develop certain traits while more submissive ones will develop others. There are many traditional choices, usually relating to size and strength, but you are free to decide how each side will be represented. You may also add more than two castes.
Holy Kingdom
Religion and politics are meant to work hand in hand. After all, religion guides the people and government leads. Why shouldn't they do so in tandem? This world will have all governments inextricably tied to the churches of their nation. This can vary from full theocracies to religious advisors serving at important levels of power.
