New Holy Land Divine Capacity: 180
This is one of the single most divine places in the multiverse.
This place can be absolutely filled with Goddesses working at their maximum ability. You barely need to make any choices.

Hierarchy Pantheon
They are still one group, but they are not all equal. The God-desses will be organized into tiers with those of higher tiers having greater power, influence, and worshiped more fervently.
You may decide the number and population of each tier.

Intertwined
Separation is for divorcees. The Goddesses walk among the mortals in this world and take a very direct role in leading the world and their followers' lives. The Holy Realm(s) still exist, but they are places on the planet that even mortals can potentially reach rather than seperate planes.
