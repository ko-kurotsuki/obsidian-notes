# Warlock
Casting trait: Charge caster
A warlock cannot cast spells unless they have gathered charges to do so through specific actions of devotion which are listed under their selected patron.
Minor charges cover spell from capacity 1 to 4, significant cover from 5 to 8, and capacity 9 and 10 spells require a major charge. All warlock spells are cast instantly.

Personal Trait: Patron
A warlock must select a patron. They gain the warlock bonus for that patron.

The Daughter of Lilith
The queen of succubi is a surprisingly common patron. Her desire is only to increase her own personal power... and the easiest way for her to do that is to increase the number of mortals doing the deed - promoting lust and providing a feeding ground for the succubi under her command. As such, all of her "gifts" are designed to futher that goal.

Trait bonus: Charm
Your caster girl is now able to cause a target to develop both a crush and an insatiable lust for someone that your caster girl specifies. Once the target has sex with the specified individual, the effect gradually fades.

Drawback effect Sluttification
Your caster girl is entirely unable to refuse sexual advances, regardless of who they come from.

Warlock bonus: Sexual prowess
Your caster girl becomes supernaturally good at sex, leagues beyond what normal mortals can aquire regardless of how much experience they get. She can play the human body's pleasure centres like a finely tuned instrument.
Warlock minor charge: Masturbation until completion.
Warlock significant charge: Having sex for at least one hour.
Warlock major charge: Take part in an orgy of at least three beings other than herself.

# traits
Wealthy
Your caster girl is particularly wealthy in some fashion. Belonging to a stupidly rich family, being a previous lottery winner, owning highly successful business ventures. The two of you will never have financial problems.

Cross class ( summoner)
Your caster girl may have one additional personal trait from any other class.
Personal trait: Harem maker
Magic or no, a summoner knows how to ingratiate herself to (or manipulate) others. Within a short span of time she will gather a harem that she's willing to share with you.
You can specify the types of harem members you want, though this will make it take much longer to gather said harem.
Patron
One of the patrons has taken a shine to your caster girl... or owed her a favour. Select a patron (if other options make you select a patron, then it must be the same patron as all other options), your caster girl gains the trait benefit of that patron.

Planeswalker
(cannot be druid)
Your caster girl can open gateways between your world and others, no longer limited to her native world. You can travel to any fictional world but each reality can only support its own rules plus your caster girl's magic... so bringing back items or skills that rely on things that don't exist in your world becomes pointless.

drawbacks
Weak
(cannot be adept)
For all her mystic might, your caster girl has zero physical aptitude. Poor manual dexterity, nearly as weak as a kitten and takes a week or so to get over even light colds.

paths

Creation
The path of creation focuses on healing and constructive aspects of magic without any other affinity and this is a strict guideline. It cannot be used directly to destroy even bacteria... though the rule does not apply to secondary effects.
Perk: Creator
Your caster girl becomes better at creating things; sculptures, art, children, food, you name it
Fetishes: loli/shota, excessive fluids, excessive sex toys
Capacities: Pro, tra, spi
Closed path: destruction

Fire

it
Perk: Consume capacities
Your caster girl can now 'burn' her magical capacities to drive her physical capabilities to superhuman levels for a few minutes. Burned capacities recover at a rate of one per week.
Fetishes: rough sex, rape, brain drain
Capacities: Con, Des, tra
Closed Path: water
Example spells: immediately and perfectly cooking a
gourmet meal from the basic ingredients (Tra: 3), incinerating incriminating physical evidence (Des 3), Sparking inspiration in someone for a day (Men 4), preventing someone from freezing to death while exposed in the arctic (Pro 4), Inciting burning hatred in a group of hundreds for a single symbol that is otherwise unrelated to them (Con 9)

Darkness
Dark magic isn't evil, but can be easily used for it. It focuses on the concepts of emptiness, obfuscation, and the absence of light or energy, and dreams... and, of course, the physical effects of darkness
Perk: Pass without trace
Your caster girl no longer leaves evidence of her presence; no foot prints, genetic evidence, alarms, or electronic recordings of her can exist.
Fetishes: sensory deprivation, somnophilia, dream invasion
Capacities: Men, con, pro
Closed path: Light
Example Spells: Making herself unnoticeable to people
she doesn't know (con 5), making her shadow independent and able to manipulate physical objects (spi 5), Invading someone's dreams (men 3), hiding herself from being detected by magic (pro 4), creating a replacement arm from tangible darkness (Tra 5), causing a dedicated person to become apathetic about a cause they love (Men 7)

Necromancy
Necromancy is, contrary to the roots of the name, the art of life; transferring it, strengthening it, taking it away, creating it where there was none to start with. Its strength lies in changing the natural order of life and death.
Perk: Ascension
If your caster girl dies, even if it is unexpected and sudden, she'll return to life as an undead of a type of your choosing (e.g. zombie, vampire, lich, ghost)
Fetishes: impregnation,, objectification
Capacities: Pro, Des, tra
Closed Path: essence
Example spells; supporting a living bodies critical processes to allow it to function through damage (pro 3) brining three dead bodies to life as zombies (spi 4), ripping the life out of a living being (des 4), causing wounds to seal themselves shut (Tra 3), Taking control of ghosts four ghosts for fifteen minutes (Men 4), creating a feeling of dread in those around her (Con 4)


Light
Light magic is based both around the physical properties of light and the concepts of illumination; truth, positivity, and guidance... though not necessarily 'good,' as its nature operates the same whether it causes harm or not
Perk: Extrasensory perception Your caster girl gains the ability to pick up on the surface thoughts of those near her.
Fetishes: free use, normalisation
Capacities: Men, con, des
Closed Path: Darkness
Example spells: Making herself invisible (tra 5) blinding a group of attackers with a flash of light (Des 4), tell if someone is lying for the duration of a conversation (men 3), summoning an angelic helper (spi 6), making a small group of people think well of someone they don't know (con 6), create a solid wall of light to stop projectiles (pro 3)

Destruction
Destruction magic, like creation magic, is without affinity elemental or otherwise. It destroys via disintegration, turning something into a true nothing... or at least speeding the process of entropic decay.
Perk: Vanquisher
Your caster girl gains an innate talent for combat of all varieties.
Fetishes:  masochism, 
Capacities: Des, tra, spi
Closed path: Creation
Example spells: dismantle a hand held item (tra 1), destroying someone's sense of self (men 7), disintegrating a living being (Des 5), destroying another caster's spell (spi 5), disintegrating a malignant tumor (pro 4), destroy someone's relationship with a friend (con 6)
