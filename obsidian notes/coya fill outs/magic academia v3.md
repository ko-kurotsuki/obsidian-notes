RANCHER
You managed to eam enough from your days as a monster rancher to pay your way into a magical education, Play through the "Ranches of Geolocus" while taking the "saving for an education reward. Not only do you have your ranch as a home, along with all rewards, but you can summon any of your ranch residents as though you had "Paradigm pact"
and "Paradigm: Vassalage" without having to pay a cost. These paradigms will be covered in spell selection.
