
Inferno
The majority of your realm thrives in the heat of fiery blazes and pyromancy. You and your minions are attuned to fire, giving you increased damage, plus the ability to resist intense heat and flames.
Smoke, heat and fire are rampant in your realm and they serve as natural, and deadly defenses.

Dark
A cloak of ominous darkness shrouds your realm, claiming the land as your own. You and your minions are attuned to shadows, giving you increased stealth and the ability to become invisible in the shadows.
Darkness realms usually feature cold, shrouded and mountainous environments perfect for paranoia.
